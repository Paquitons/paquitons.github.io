About Iron Volt Electric

A licensed Houston electrical contractor, built on 25 years of field work.

Where the company came from

Iron Volt Electric was founded on 12 January 2026 by a licensed electrician who had spent 25 years working in Houston homes, businesses, parks and community properties.

That career, thousands of jobs across every kind of residential and commercial project, is what the company is built on.

"Iron" is durability. "Volt" is the electricity. Together they describe what we are trying to deliver: work that lasts.

We are not a call centre or a franchise. We are a local Houston electrician who knows the local codes, the way these houses were built, and what the Houston climate does to an electrical system over twenty years.

Licensed, bonded and insured

What we carry, and what it protects you against.

  • TECL #41098. Texas Electrical Contractor Licence, issued and maintained by the TDLR. Verifiable at any time.
  • Fully insured. General liability and workers' compensation cover on every job, with proof provided before work starts.
  • Bonded. A surety bond providing additional protection for customers.
  • Code compliant. All work performed to current NEC standards and local Houston codes, with permits pulled and inspections booked.

What we hold to

  • Safety without shortcuts

    Every job is performed to NEC code. Electrical work done badly is dangerous for your family, your property and for us. We use quality materials and take the time the job needs.

  • Straight pricing

    You get a written estimate before work begins. If you don't need the expensive option, we say so. No upselling and no vague numbers.

  • Two decades of Houston houses

    Knob-and-tube, aluminium wiring, modern smart systems, storm damage. Our lead electrician has worked on all of it, here, for over twenty years.

  • Respect for your time

    We turn up when we said we would, work efficiently and leave the place clean.
