Why it matters that we're local

  • We get there faster

    Being based in the metro means we reach most of it quickly, which is the whole point when a breaker is arcing.

  • We know the local codes

    Houston and the surrounding municipalities each have their own electrical requirements. Knowing them is how work passes inspection first time.

  • We know these houses

    Houston housing stock has patterns: aluminium wiring in the 60s and 70s builds, undersized panels in the 80s, storm damage everywhere.

  • Word of mouth is our marketing

    Most of our work comes from neighbours recommending us, which is a strong incentive not to cut corners on your job.
